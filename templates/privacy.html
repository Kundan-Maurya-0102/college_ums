Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ PRIVACY_LAST_UPDATED }} · Applies to the {{ COLLEGE_NAME|default:"College UMS" }} portal.

{{ COLLEGE_NAME|default:"The college" }} is the data controller for this portal. We collect only the student and staff information needed to run academic and administrative work, and we do not sell it.

1. What we collect

  • Identity and contact: name, registration/enrolment number, branch, semester, roll number, college email.
  • Optional contact: personal phone number, postal address and parent/guardian names — only if you or the office provides them.
  • Academic records: attendance, internal and exam marks, assignment and study-material activity, leave and feedback submissions.
  • Financial records: fee structure, payments, receipts and dues.
  • Technical data: login timestamps, pages visited on the portal, and IP address (used for security and for the admin usage dashboard).

2. Why we use it

  • To deliver the portal: sign-in, attendance, results, fee receipts, notices and certificates.
  • To communicate college notices and (where enabled) fee reminders on WhatsApp/SMS/email.
  • To keep the campus and the portal secure, and to investigate misuse.
  • To meet legal, audit and accreditation requirements.

3. Who can see it

  • You see your own profile, marks, attendance and fee records.
  • Teachers see the students of the classes assigned to them, plus marks and attendance they enter.
  • College administrators (office/accounts/exam cell) see the records needed for their role.
  • Platform operator may see aggregate health/usage data and support tickets; a named administrator's data export requires your college's approval and is audit-logged.

Every admin action on personal data (edit, export, anonymise/delete, bulk import) is written to an audit log with user, time and IP.

4. Vendors we may use

  • Email/SMS/WhatsApp gateway — to deliver notices and reminders. Only the contact number/address and message text are shared.
  • Online fee payments — this portal currently records fee payments and generates receipts entered by the accounts office. If an online payment gateway is enabled in the future, the gateway will process the payment; the portal will receive only a transaction reference, never your card or UPI credentials.
  • Hosting and error monitoring — server logs and crash reports. Personal data is redacted before logs leave the server.

5. How long we keep it

Academic and fee records are retained for {{ DATA_RETENTION_YEARS }} years after a student's last enrolment, or for the period required by the college and its regulator, whichever is longer. After that, personal identifiers are anonymised and the remaining statistics are kept. Audit logs are kept for the same period.

6. Your choices

  • You can edit your own contact details in Profile; fields marked optional can be left blank.
  • You can ask the college office for a copy of your data (JSON/CSV export) or for correction.
  • You can ask for your personal data to be deleted/anonymised after you leave the college; academic records needed for certification are kept but de-identified where possible.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, admin pages are role-restricted, uploads are type-checked, database backups are encrypted, and access to production data is limited to named administrators. Report a security issue to {{ COLLEGE_EMAIL|default:"the college office" }}.
